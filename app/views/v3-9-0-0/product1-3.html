    Flood zones

    Flood zones show the chance of flooding from rivers and the sea, ignoring flood defences. They are used to decide whether a flood risk assessment is needed.

    About this data

    Flood zone 3 has a 1% or greater chance each year of flooding from rivers, or a 0.5% or greater chance from the sea. Flood zone 2 has between a 0.1% and 1% chance from rivers, or between 0.1% and 0.5% from the sea.

    Source: Environment Agency, National Flood Risk Assessment (NaFRA2). The data shown is correct on the date this summary was created.

    Surface water, present day

    Surface water flooding happens when heavy rain cannot drain away. It is not part of the flood zones, so it does not change whether a flood risk assessment is needed, but you may need to consider it in one. The map shows areas with a 1 in 100 (1%) chance of flooding each year.

    There is a risk of surface water flooding inside your site boundary, up to 300mm deep.
